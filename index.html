About

Hello, I'm Hao (Hank), MSc student at UofA, advised by Dr. Ngan Le. I work on 4D scene understanding, where I'm interested in bringing geometric and temporal structure into models that learn from multi-view images and video, moving perception off the image plane and into space and time, with an eye toward robotics and autonomous driving.

Looking ahead, I want to explore how to give machines a grounded understanding of the physical world and enable them to work together, advancing from task-specific systems toward general-purpose, collaborative autonomy.
